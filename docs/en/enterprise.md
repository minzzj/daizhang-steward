## 2. Key capabilities and safety defaults

- **Four entity checks**: business license / unified social credit code, company authorization letter
  (**human-signed**), corporate bank account and tax number; any missing or unsigned item blocks the order.
- **Dual review**: account or sensitive changes above `review_policy.dual_review_threshold_cny`
  (5000 in the fixture) only take effect after 2 approvals from **different roles** (finance, legal);
  one approval leaves the request `pending_dual_review`.
- **Org approval chain**: amount bands (fixture: 3000 → 1 level, 20000 → 2 levels, 80000 → 3 levels);
  the AI only submits for approval and never escalates on its own.
- **Seal**: `seal-flow.json` goes `initial.status=pending_human` → `signed.status=signed`; `ai_may_sign=false`
  holds throughout and `signed_by=示例法人（虚构，人签）`.
- **Invoices**: `invoice-validation.json` reports `title_matches/tax_no_matches/contract_matches` all true,
  with `draft_status=draft_pending_human` — the AI only drafts, it never issues.
- **Tax filing**: drafts only (`ai_may_submit=false`); finance/the owner must review and submit manually.
- **Employment / dispatch / qualifications**: EB-26/EB-27 hand off to a human; the AI never recruits, signs
  or promises qualification outcomes.
- **Multi-agent serialization**: `multi_agent_serial=true` — a local serial queue avoids concurrency
  conflicts and privilege overlap.

## 6. Safety boundaries and known limitations

- **All data fictional**: license number, tax number, bank account and the legal representative/agent names
  are invented (marked fictional) and only for local acceptance; never use them for real signing, payments,
  invoicing or tax filing.
- **No real systems connected**: no real bank, tax, CA or e-signature platform; invoices and tax filings are
  **drafts only**; seals only record the "human signed" state and an audit trail, no real seal file is generated.
- **The AI never signs, never issues invoices, never submits filings and never changes accounts on its own**;
  org approval chains and dual review are executed by humans.
- **Multi-agent/multi-account serialization**: enterprise mode uses a local serial queue
  (`multi_agent_serial`); cross-account actions need human confirmation; no distributed concurrency
  scheduler is implemented.
- **No enterprise-specific UI yet**: the console page reuses the personal-edition console (P1); enterprise
  data is exercised through the CLI/fixtures. Enterprise dashboards/workflow UI are future work.
- The 30 rules cover the programmatically enforceable scope of the enterprise catalog; enterprise-specific
  processes (authorization-letter drafting, real stamping, local tax-rule differences) still need human
  review and are not a substitute for legal/tax advice.
- Version: P3 delivers **v0.8.0** (P2 was v0.7.0); GitHub Release/tag is handled by the Lead — the actual
  `package.json` version in the repository is authoritative.
