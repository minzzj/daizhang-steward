# Overview (search-keyword friendly)

> Steward (Daizhang Steward) is a **local-first AI Company OS**: a conversational AI controller dispatches a team of AI agents that automate real work — visible thinking, verifiable artifacts, humans only approve.

## What it is (one line)

**AI Company OS**: give one goal → the AI controller decomposes and dispatches → multiple AI employees (agents) execute in isolated contexts (real browser/desktop operations, coding, research, finance, customer comms, content, reports) → real files/reports/data are produced → humans only approve risky actions.

## Who it is for

- Small teams/individuals who want **AI employees to automate operations and office work**;
- People who need AI that **actually executes** (real browser/desktop automation + real artifacts + auditability), not just chat;
- Teams wanting **local-first, private AI automation** with data staying on the machine;
- Multi-agent orchestration without context pollution: each agent has its own workspace and memory.
